Edge of Normal

Shorts Autopilot is a private publishing tool used only by the creator of the Edge of Normal channels. It uploads the creator's own short videos to the creator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts after the creator approves each video.

The tool is not offered to the public. No other people can sign in to it, and it does not collect data about viewers or other users.

YouTube

Uploads to the YouTube channel Edge of Normal on YouTube are made with the YouTube API Services. By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; YouTube data is handled as described in our Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.
